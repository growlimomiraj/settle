Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, cruise shuttles, airport transfers, and private charters with Seattle Express Shuttle.

1. How much is the cruise shuttle?

$30 per person, each way, between Sea-Tac Airport or any Sea-Tac area hotel and Pier 66 or Pier 91. Children 3 and under ride free on an adult’s lap.

2. Which pier does my cruise ship use?

Your cruise booking paperwork specifies your departure terminal:

  • Pier 66 (Bell Street Cruise Terminal): Downtown Seattle on Alaskan Way – Norwegian Cruise Line (NCL) and Oceania Cruises.
  • Pier 91 (Smith Cove Cruise Terminal): Magnolia on W Garfield St – Princess Cruises, Holland America Line, Celebrity Cruises, Royal Caribbean, and Carnival Cruise Line.

3. Can I book a ride back from the cruise pier?

Yes. Select “From the cruise terminal” on our booking form and enter your airport or hotel as the drop-off destination. Our dispatch team coordinates post-cruise disembarkation pickups smoothly.

4. How do I pay for my ride?

Your transparent price is displayed before you submit your reservation. We confirm every booking by email and phone with all details. We accept all major credit cards.

5. Do you offer private rides and charter transfers?

Yes. Choose “Personal Trip” for a private Sedan (up to 3 passengers), Luxury SUV (up to 5 passengers), Minivan (up to 8 passengers), or Luxury Mercedes Sprinter (up to 14 passengers), priced by distance plus 15% gratuity.

6. Where do I meet my driver at SeaTac Airport?

Standard pickup is at SeaTac Airport parking garage Level 3. Meet and Greet service inside baggage claim is also available upon request.

7. How do I change or cancel a booking?

Call us directly at (206) 504-6000 or email info@seattleexpressshuttle.com. For rides within the next 24 hours, please call directly.

8. What is your luggage allowance?

On shared cruise shuttles, each passenger is allowed one standard large checked bag and one personal carry-on item. If you have extra bags, please note it during booking so we can plan vehicle capacity.
