Frequently Asked Questions

Seattle Express Shuttle FAQ

How much is the cruise shuttle?

$30 per person, each way, between Sea-Tac Airport or any Sea-Tac area hotel and Pier 66 or Pier 91. Children 3 and under ride free on an adult’s lap and do not need their own ticket.

Which pier does my cruise ship use?

Your cruise documents confirm your terminal. Pier 66 is the Bell Street Cruise Terminal in downtown Seattle (serving Norwegian and Oceania Cruises). Pier 91 is the Smith Cove Cruise Terminal in Magnolia (serving Celebrity, Holland America, Princess, Royal Caribbean, and Carnival).

Can I book a ride back from the cruise pier?

Yes. Choose “From the cruise terminal” in our online booking form and enter your Sea-Tac hotel or SeaTac airport as the drop-off destination. We coordinate return disembarkation pickups seamlessly.

How do I pay for my booking?

Your all-inclusive price is confirmed before you submit. We confirm every reservation by email and phone with complete itinerary details. All major credit cards are accepted with zero hidden fees or surge pricing.

Do you offer private rides and charter transfers?

Yes. Choose “Personal Trip” for a private Sedan (up to 3 passengers), Luxury SUV (up to 5 passengers), Minivan (up to 8 passengers), or Luxury Mercedes Sprinter (up to 14 passengers), priced by distance plus 15% gratuity.

Where do I meet my driver at SeaTac Airport?

Standard pickup is located at the SeaTac Airport parking garage Level 3. Meet and Greet service is also available inside at baggage claim with a personalized chauffeur name sign. We track all inbound flights in real time.

Which Sea-Tac hotels do you pick up from?

We pick up at all hotels near Sea-Tac Airport, including our partner hotels: DoubleTree by Hilton Seattle Airport, Radisson Hotel Seattle Airport, Four Points by Sheraton, and all other airport & downtown hotels.

How do I change or cancel a booking?

To change or cancel your ride, call us directly at (206) 504-6000 or email info@seattleexpressshuttle.com. For trips within the next 24 hours, please call us directly.
